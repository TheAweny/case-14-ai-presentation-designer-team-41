Show the person a deck outline in a card: `title`, `audience`, `goal` and 5 to 60
`slides`, each with a `title` that states the slide's claim, up to eight short `points`, and a
`kind` (cover, section, statement, list, cards, process, comparison, problem-solution, team,
closing, reference, other). `templateId` is the template you mean to build it in, or null when
it is not chosen yet. A new proposal replaces the previous one. Returns the `planId`.

Each figure of the material goes to one slide, the one whose claim it proves; only the closing
slide may sum figures up again. A point states only what the material says or what follows from
it plainly: a result the material does not report (payback, customer satisfaction, market share)
is not a point.

When the person names a length, keep to it: a range means its upper end, and never fewer than
10 slides. Section dividers are for decks of 15 slides or more; a shorter deck spends every
slide on content.
